    Syntax and limits

    Nothing runs as code. Expressions go through a small bounded arithmetic parser, not JavaScript, so names like alert are just unknown.

    • Precedence, high to low: ( ) and calls, postfix !, then ^ (or **), then unary -, then * / %, then + -. % is the remainder.
    • ^ is right-associative and binds tighter than a leading minus: 2^3^2 is 2^9 = 512 and -2^2 is -4. Write (-2)^2 for 4.
    • Literals: decimals and exponents (1.5e3), hex 0xff, binary 0b1010 and octal 0o17. Multiplication is always explicit, so 2pi is an error; write 2*pi.
    • BigInt: add n to an integer (2n^100n, 0xffn) for exact arithmetic. Don't mix BigInt with ordinary numbers; BigInt division truncates. abs sign min max accept BigInt, and 30n! is exact.
    • Floats: results show 15 significant digits, so 0.1+0.2 reads 0.3. The exact binary value appears below it whenever the two differ.
    • Functions: sqrt cbrt exp ln log log10 log2 · sin cos tan asin acos atan atan2(y, x) · sinh cosh tanh · abs sign floor ceil round trunc · pow(x, y) hypot(…) min(…) max(…). log and ln are both natural logs.
    • Constants: pi, tau (2π) and e. DEG/RAD changes what trig functions take and what inverse trig returns; in DEG, multiples of 90° are exact.
    • Limits: 2,000 characters, nesting 100 deep, BigInt results up to 20,000 bits (exponents 0–20,000), 170! for numbers and 2000n! for BigInt. Division by zero, non-real results and overflow are errors, not Infinity or NaN.
