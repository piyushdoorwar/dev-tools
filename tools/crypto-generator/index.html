      Everything runs in your browser. Password generation, hashing, and copy actions stay local on your device.

      The strength analyser is a compact pattern-matching estimator in the spirit of zxcvbn, run entirely in this page: it looks for common passwords, dictionary words, l33t swaps, keyboard walks, sequences, repeats and dates, then estimates how many guesses a cracker would need.
