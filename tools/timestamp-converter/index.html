Tips

  • Digit count decides the unit: 10 digits is seconds, 13 is milliseconds, 16 is microseconds.
  • A bare date like 2026-09-18 is read as midnight UTC, matching ISO 8601.
  • The picker reads and writes in the selected time zone, so daylight-saving shifts are applied.
