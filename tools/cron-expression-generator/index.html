Syntax Reference

Fields

┌───────────── minute        0-59
│ ┌─────────── hour          0-23
│ │ ┌───────── day of month  1-31
│ │ │ ┌─────── month         1-12 or JAN-DEC
│ │ │ │ ┌───── day of week   0-6  or SUN-SAT (7 = Sunday)
│ │ │ │ │
* * * * *

Operators

  • * every value · 1,3 a list · 1-5 a range · */15 a step · 9-17/2 a stepped range.
  • A sixth leading field is read as seconds, so */30 * * * * * runs twice a minute.
  • Macros: @yearly @monthly @weekly @daily @hourly @reboot.

Tips

  • When day-of-month and day-of-week are both restricted, cron runs on days matching either one — not both.
  • Next runs are computed on the selected time zone's wall clock, so a daily job keeps its local hour across daylight-saving changes. Times that do not exist in a spring-forward gap are skipped.
  • L, W and # are Quartz extensions that standard cron does not accept, so they are reported as errors here.
