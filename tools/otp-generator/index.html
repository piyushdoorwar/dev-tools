How it works

Codes

  • HOTP (RFC 4226) signs an 8-byte counter with HMAC and truncates the result to a 6–8 digit code. Both sides advance the counter on every use.
  • TOTP (RFC 6238) is HOTP whose counter is the number of whole periods since the Unix epoch, floor(time / 30) by default.
  • Previous and next show the neighbouring steps, which most servers also accept to absorb clock drift.

Secret

  • Secrets are Base32 (A–Z and 2–7). Spaces, lower case and missing = padding are accepted.
  • Generate secret draws 20 random bytes (160 bits, the RFC 4226 recommendation) from the browser's cryptographic generator.
  • Paste a full otpauth:// URI to fill every field at once.

Provisioning

  • The URI follows the Google Authenticator key-uri format; scan the QR with an authenticator app to enrol.
  • Some apps ignore the algorithm, digits and period parameters and always use SHA-1, 6 digits and 30 s.

Everything is calculated in your browser with WebCrypto; the secret never leaves the page.
